This shows the actual close-kin pairs observed in the sample (8 parent–offspring dyads, 7 full-sibling families) — a descriptive illustration of the links found, not a modelled dispersal or migration network. Sample coordinates are site-level; where multiple individuals share one site, points are nudged slightly apart on screen so they can be seen — this visual jitter does not change any reported distance, which is always calculated from the real coordinates.
